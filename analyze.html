How is Z-score calculated?

Z = (your mark − class average) ÷ standard deviation. This is the same formula used for the A/L Z-score in Sri Lanka. Z = 0 means exactly average. Z = +1 means one standard deviation above the average. Your Z-score is calculated against every registered student of your A/L year who has a mark for that paper.
